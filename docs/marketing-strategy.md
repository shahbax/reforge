# Reforge — Marketing Strategy (Solo-Founder Playbook)

_A concrete, low-budget go-to-market you can run yourself. Companion to `go-live-plan.md` and `viability-verdict.md`._

## 1. Core message (say this everywhere)
**"Reverse-engineer why a video went viral — then create something original that survives YouTube's 2026 inauthentic-content rules."**

- Primary hook (fear + relief): *avoid demonetization / reused-content flags* by creating genuinely original content, with an originality score to prove it.
- Secondary hook (speed): go from a viral video → a ready-to-film script in minutes, not hours.
- Never say "copy videos." Always: *understand the mechanics, create original.*

## 2. Who you're selling to (in priority order)
1. **Faceless-channel operators** starting/scaling — highest volume, highest anxiety about demonetization.
2. **Established small creators** who want a repeatable ideation→script system.
3. **Small agencies / freelancers** making content for clients (higher willingness to pay, lower churn).
4. **Repurposers / marketers** turning trends into original short-form.

Broaden beyond #1 early — they churn most (they quit YouTube). #3 and #4 are your retention anchors.

## 3. Channels (ranked by ROI for a solo founder, ~$0 budget)
### A. SEO (primary long-term engine)
- **Free tool as lead magnet:** a public "Hook Analyzer" or "Viral Title Analyzer" (no login) → captures search traffic, funnels to signup. (Highest-leverage single build.)
- **Programmatic pages:** "YouTube script generator for {niche}", "{niche} video ideas", "faceless {niche} channel guide" — one template, many niches.
- **Cornerstone blog posts** targeting real intent: *how to avoid YouTube's inauthentic-content policy, faceless YouTube in 2026, how to reverse-engineer a viral video, best AI script tools.* Each links to the app + free tool.
- Technical SEO is already shipped (metadata, sitemap, robots, JSON-LD). After deploy: submit sitemap to **Google Search Console** + **Bing Webmaster**.

### B. Communities (fastest early traction)
- **Reddit:** r/NewTubers, r/youtubers, r/PartneredYoutube, faceless/automation subs. Give value first (free breakdowns), mention tool sparingly. Watch each sub's self-promo rules.
- **Discords / Skool:** YouTube-automation and faceless-channel servers. Offer free analyses, gather feedback, convert power users.
- **X/Twitter + TikTok "build in public":** post real Viral-DNA breakdowns of trending videos (great shareable content that literally demos the product).

### C. Launch moments
- **Product Hunt** launch (prep assets, hunter, first-100 list). Aim for a top-5 day.
- **AppSumo / lifetime deal** later if you want a cash injection (Subscribr did $79 LTD) — optional, dilutes MRR.

### D. Partnerships / influencers
- Micro-YouTubers who teach faceless automation: give free Pro + an affiliate cut for tutorials/reviews. Their audience *is* your ICP.

## 4. Conversion & retention
- **Onboarding** (shipped): 3-step explainer + "Try an example" so value lands in <60s. Add a first-run sample result.
- **Free → paid:** free = 2 analyses; paywall triggers at the moment of value (after they've seen a great script). Show credits + a soft upgrade nudge.
- **Retention = a weekly habit:** email a "trending in your niche → analyze it" nudge; ship channel-level analysis (V2) so it's a recurring workflow, not one-and-done.
- **Reduce churn from the 97% who quit YouTube:** lean into agencies/marketers, and add a "repurpose your own long-form" use case so value isn't tied to becoming a YouTube star.

## 5. 90-day launch sequence
**Weeks 0–2 (pre-launch):** deploy; enable real AI; ship the free tool + 5–10 SEO pages; set up Search Console, PostHog, Sentry; seed 20–30 great breakdowns on X/Reddit; build a waitlist/first-100 list.
**Weeks 3–4 (launch):** Product Hunt + community launch; DM waitlist; publish 2 cornerstone posts; collect testimonials.
**Weeks 5–12 (compound):** 2–3 SEO pieces/week; weekly PH-style updates; onboard agencies; iterate onboarding on real funnel data; start affiliate outreach.

## 6. Funnel metrics to watch (tie to kill/continue criteria in `viability-verdict.md`)
- Traffic → signup rate; **activation** (% completing one analysis→script); **free→paid** (target 5–10%/30d); W4 retention; CAC (keep ~$0 via SEO/community); gross margin per plan.

## 7. Budget (lean)
- Month 1: ~$10–15 infra + $0 marketing (organic only) + optional ~$50 for a domain/logo/PH assets.
- Scale spend only *after* organic proves activation + conversion. Avoid paid ads until LTV is known (churn risk makes early paid CAC dangerous).

## 8. Positioning guardrails (legal/brand)
- Always: originality analysis is an AI assessment, **not** legal advice or a monetization guarantee.
- Market the *capability* ("create original content") not a *promise* ("won't get demonetized").
